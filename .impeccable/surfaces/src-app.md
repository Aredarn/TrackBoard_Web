Scope: the whole TrackBoard web app (public tracks, leaderboards, driver pages; signed-in driver area; account). Visitor mode: Operate. Audience: TrackPro drivers reviewing a track day at home; second, strangers arriving on a shared leaderboard link.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Results as the timekeeper posts them. Every leaderboard is an official classification sheet pinned to the paddock board, stamped PROVISIONAL because times are self-reported. Refuses the dark racing dashboard with a red timing tower.

OWN-WORLD: Felt-green noticeboard ground; bright paper sheets with hard ink rules; violet rubber-stamp ink is the one accent (overall best, primary action, stamps); green only for PB; highlighter yellow marks your own row. Dymo-tape nav labels, typed Courier metadata, condensed Archivo figures. Night renders sheets as carbon copies.

STORY: See where every lap placed, find your highlighted line, see the gap and sector, open the driver or session behind it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Track page: sheet header with circuit diagram drawn from its points, meta, lap-record stamp; classification table starts above the fold.
